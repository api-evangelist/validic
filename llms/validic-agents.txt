# Validic Developer Agent Guide

You are here because a developer wants to build with Validic health data APIs.
There are two paths. Pick the one that matches their situation.

## Path A  --  Already have credentials
The developer has a Validic account and knows their ORG_ID and ORG_TOKEN.
Load: inform-quickstart.txt

## Path B  --  Starting from scratch
The developer does not have a Validic account yet. They need to register,
verify their email, and provision an org before they can call any APIs.
Load: validic-developer-signup.txt

If you are unsure which applies, ask the developer whether they have an ORG_ID
and ORG_TOKEN. That is the only question that matters.
